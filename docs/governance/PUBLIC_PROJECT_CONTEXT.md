## General public-safe working principles

1. **Discover before creating.** Understand the original product/user need, existing code, rights and current owner decisions before adding functionality or documents.
2. **Research value, not buzzwords.** Assess usability, real demand, useful comparable patterns, distribution, maintenance, pricing and total cost before proposing expansion. Do not promise commercial success based on an idea or UI mockup.
3. **Build meaningful verified work.** Separate concept, implementation, passing tests, verified device/runtime behavior, deployment and customer acceptance; do not call one equivalent to another.
4. **Preserve owners and rights.** Maintain original project identity, provenance, third-party licenses, user privacy and platform/app-store rules. Do not copy private enterprise materials into a public repository.
5. **Be transparent on limitations.** Identify incomplete features, simulated content, external dependencies, known failures and release holds. Never fabricate live status, payments, customers or launch readiness.
6. **Keep original decisions recoverable.** Use existing project documentation, tests, issue/PR history and a local current-state summary; do not create another authoritative business office or overwrite unrelated work.
7. **Scope and review before consequential change.** Explicit approval is needed for publishing, billing/payment integration, customer-data handling, licensing or other potentially consequential actions.
