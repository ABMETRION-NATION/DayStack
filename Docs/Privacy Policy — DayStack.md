Privacy Policy — DayStack

Effective Date: April 2026

DayStack (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application currently handles information.

1. Information Collection

DayStack does not require account creation and does not collect personally identifiable information.

The app may store basic task-related data locally on your device to provide core functionality.

2. Data Storage

All task data is stored locally on your device.
We do not currently store user data on external servers.

3. Third-Party Services

DayStack does not currently use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

4. Future Features

Future versions of DayStack may introduce:

optional account login (e.g., Google or Facebook)
cloud synchronization
analytics
advertising
in-app purchases

If and when these features are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share user data with third parties.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user data. Since data is stored locally, security depends in part on your device protections.

7. Children’s Privacy

DayStack is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as features evolve. Updates will be reflected with a revised effective date.
